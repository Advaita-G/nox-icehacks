    Physics model and limits

    Real 3D rigid bodies. Uniform solid spheres: I = 2mr²/5. Contact impulses, friction, gravity and rotation use Rapier 0.21.0 at 120 steps/s. Recorded samples: 30 Hz.

    One Coulomb coefficient, arithmetic mean contact mixing. Declared air drag, forces, joint constraints, springs and finite-effort motors. Fan airflow uses an ideal actuator-disk approximation with explicit parameters. No deformation or rolling resistance. Fixed supports, gravity and edits exchange energy and momentum. Grid lines are a reference, not an invisible floor.

    128 bodies / 512 shape parts, 30 s/run. Dimensions 0.1 to 30 m, mass 0.05 to 100 kg, speed ≤50 m/s, spin ≤80 rad/s. Extreme contacts pause at safety limits. Contact solving is approximate; overlapping starts may add energy.
